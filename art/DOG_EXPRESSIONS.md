# 犬の追加アニメーション素材

2026-10-04。ホーム画面の動きを増やす指示を受け、built-in imagegenで写真参照犬の表情sheetを制作した。既存の犬画像は維持。原稿は `source/dog-expressions-source.png`、配信用は `dog-expressions.png` と `dog-expressions.json`。生成後のCanvas処理はサイズ正規化・alpha領域と足元anchorの計測だけ。

## 生成プロンプト

Use case: identity-preserve. Asset type: transparent game sprite atlas with eight individual front-view dog expressions. Input image 1 is the established game character's front atlas: preserve that exact dog's identity, proportions and fur look. Input image 2 is original photographic identity reference; preserve its cinnamon/orange long fluffy coat, white forehead blaze and chest, large dark brown round eyes, short brown-pink nose, delicate face and curled plume tail. Create ONE square transparent sheet, exactly 4 equally sized columns and 2 equally sized rows, eight full-body isolated dogs, precisely one centered dog per cell, all at same frontal camera angle, scale and paw baseline, roomy clear transparent gutters. Row 1 left-to-right: neutral happy panting standing; both eyes gently closed blinking with same smile; head angled just slightly to its left inquisitively; head angled slightly to its right. Row 2 left-to-right: one front paw lifted in a friendly little wave; a small playful bow with nose sniffing slightly downwards, hindquarters up but still full body; delighted smile with tongue out and tail fluff slightly displaced to left; delighted smile with tongue out and tail fluff slightly displaced to right. Same recognizable dog in every cell, natural realistic furry pet rendering matching the reference atlas, NOT a new generic Pomeranian, NOT cartoon facial eyes, no clothing or collars added. No text, numbers, borders, labels, floors, shadows or background, genuine transparent alpha. Full ears, all paws and tail inside each cell. This sheet is for natural blinking, head turns and paw motion in a portrait game home screen. Keep facial likeness and warm soft detail.

入力は既存front atlasと最初の犬写真。仕草は瞬き、左右の首かしげ、前足、遊びのポーズ、完走の2姿勢。顔や毛並みは写真を基に生成した補完表現。
